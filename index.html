01 / PROFILE

About Me

I am a banking and finance professional with more than 15 years of experience in banking, alongside university teaching, academic research, and postgraduate thesis supervision.

My professional experience includes banking operations, financial statement analysis, risk management, and financial analysis. My academic work spans accounting, auditing, corporate governance, financial markets, and quantitative research methods.

I am particularly interested in research connecting banking practice with empirical investigation of financial stability, bank distress, financial regulation, and capital markets.
